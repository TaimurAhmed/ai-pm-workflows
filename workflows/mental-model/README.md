# Mental Model Workflow 🚧

**Status:** work in progress — parked deliberately, shape captured below.

**Where it sits:** before everything — the orientation pass. Whether you're onboarding into a new company, running a case study, or walking into an interview, the same grounding applies before any specific question (analytics, execution, or design):

1. **Mission of the ecosystem** — what world does this product live in, and where is that world going?
2. **Mission of the parent company** — what is the company actually trying to achieve?
3. **Purpose of the product** — why does this product exist inside that mission?
4. **North Star** — the one metric/outcome that says the product is doing its job.
5. **Tie it together** — which side of the market should you be focusing on, and why?

**Why it matters:** this altitude applies universally — it's the difference between answering *a* question and answering it *for this company*. Do it once, and every downstream workflow (Opportunity, Ideation, Solution) inherits the context.

**What it will produce:** a one-page mental model the PM can hold in their head — reusable across every conversation that follows.
