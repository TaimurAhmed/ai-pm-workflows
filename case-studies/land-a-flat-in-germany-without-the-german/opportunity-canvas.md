## 1 · Customer opportunity — *why should the customer care?*

### 🔷 CUSTOMER OPPORTUNITY — BEST SHOT
> A renter who isn't fluent in German gets the **best possible shot** at the flat — not just the minimum a landlord asks for, but **proactive reassurance on top** (extra cover, professional watermarked docs, things they didn't even ask for) — all from one trusted profile, safe and in their control — so they **win the yes, not just clear the bar**.

*Problem + desire = the opportunity. The pains and desires below are the two halves that add up to it.*

**🩷 Pains (fears at the moment of applying)**
- Miss the deadline — too slow assembling the pack while a faster applicant takes the flat.
- Trip over a hidden, unwritten German cultural quirk.
- A required German document is missing — found out too late.
- Exposing sensitive data (passport scans) with no easy way to protect it.

**🔴 Evidence (sourced — see [`research/`](research/))**
- **Silent screening** on name / nationality / language — DeZIM: **22% vs 16%** viewing invites, plus a Federal Court ruling.
- **The SCHUFA / no-German-credit wall** — can't be generated from abroad, yet treated as mandatory.

**🟢 Desires (gains / measures of success)**
- Provide data **once, use it everywhere**.
- Each application **personalised to the landlord** → more replies.
- **Go beyond the minimum** → strongest possible case.
- *(discussed, not placed)* Feel like the **front-runner, not the underdog**.

## 2 · Empathy mapping

- **🟡 FEEL:** rushed & anxious — already mid-application, clock ticking.
- **🟡 THINK:** *"just tell me what I need — don't lecture me."*
- **🩷 PAIN:** must grasp what to prepare, but has no patience for a tutorial — help must be **just-in-time and light**.

> Lived example (the PM's own): won a flat *fast* by giving the landlord the minimum asked **and then some** — unprompted liability insurance and professionally watermarked ID scans. Over-delivery, not compliance, won the yes.

## 3 · Target user & customer cohorts

- **🔵 TARGET CUSTOMER:** a renter **not fluent in German** (e.g. an expat who just moved), applying in a stacked, fast-moving market where they start out the **underdog**.
- **🔵 COHORT A — cold start:** no flat yet → wants a **generic** Bewerbung ready to go.
- **🔵 COHORT B — mid-flow:** found a specific flat on ImmoScout → wants one **tailored to that flat, fast**. *Proposed MVP wedge — highest intent, most urgent.*

*Superset → subsets: one specific customer, sliced by the moment they arrive.*

## 4 · Solutions today

| Alternative | What it does | Gap it leaves |
|---|---|---|
| Listing portals — ImmoScout24 Plus / WG-Gesucht+ | Paid applicant profile + doc-locker (~€13–30/mo) | No persuasive or fluent-German writing |
| Templates + credit/deposit tools — Selbstauskunft, SCHUFA/bonify, Getmomo | Supply individual pieces | Not assembled; not personalised |
| Comms — portal templates, bulk-apply bots, settlyo, DeepL | Canned messages, volume, one-shot letters, translation | Generic/ToS-risky; translation ≠ credible German |
| Human relocation agents — BerlinRelo, FARAWAYHOME | End-to-end "best shot" service | €800–1,600 — priced out for solo renters |

**🟠 WHITE SPACE:** nothing turns an expat's raw facts into a **credible, personalised, fluent-German Bewerbung**. *CREATE* is the least-served job.

## 7 · Impact stack-rank — MoSCoW

Criteria (mapped back to the canvas): **Must** = lawful *and* advances the customer opportunity + business mission · **Should** = advances the opportunity + mission · **Could** = maps to a pain but not essential to what we're testing · **Won't (yet)** = consciously parked.

| Priority | How might we… | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| **MUST** | Assemble a complete, landlord-ready Bewerbung fast | The core CREATE job — the white space itself |
| **MUST** | Handle data safely & lawfully (e.g. auto-watermark IDs) | A legal/compliance floor, not a feature |
| **SHOULD** | Make it read as credible, fluent German | The differentiator vs. a doc-locker *(proposed split from "personalise")* |
| **SHOULD** | Tailor each application to the specific landlord | Lifts reply rates; personalisation as polish |
| **SHOULD** | Enter details once, reuse everywhere | High-leverage enabler for applying at scale |
| **COULD** | Nudge them to go beyond the minimum | Differentiator from the win-story; cheap to add |
| **WON'T (yet)** | Coach past the SCHUFA / no-credit wall | Thorny, partly served elsewhere; consciously parked |

## Parked Solutions (off-canvas seeds → Ideation)

Solution ideas surfaced mid-session and parked so they didn't contaminate the problem space — each carries the **underlying need** it serves:

- **Progressive onboarding = data collection** — every onboarding question quietly gathers info for the application.
- **Auto-watermark uploaded IDs** — "for Bewerbung only", privacy by default.
- **Reuse one core profile → auto-tailor per listing** — *need: a real shot despite brutal odds, without effort & anxiety multiplying per listing.*
- **Listing legitimacy / scam checker** — *need: trust the listing & person are real before spending effort or exposing data.* (Adjacent job — vetting the landlord, not making the Bewerbung.)
- **Naming (for later):** the solution's name must signal *"best shot — you'll beat the market"*, not a mere "fair shot".
