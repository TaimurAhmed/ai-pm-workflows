# Case study: Land a flat in Germany without the German

**Date:** 19 July 2026 · **Team:** Taimur Ahmed (PM) + claudi-boy 🤖❤️ (AI product team) · **Time-box:** two–three hours, here and there

## The one-liner

A full Opportunity Workflow run — from blank Miro board to a designed Opportunity Brief — for **Bewerbot 9000**: a tool that gives renters who aren't fluent in German their *best shot* at a Berlin flat. Beachhead cohort: the non-fluent expat, applying in a market with 100+ applicants per listing, starting out the underdog.

**The opportunity ("Best Shot"):** a non-fluent renter gets the best possible shot at the flat — not just the minimum a landlord asks for, but proactive reassurance on top — from one trusted profile, safe and in their control, so they win the yes rather than just clear the bar.

## How it was made (the meta-story)

Run entirely through this repo's workflows, in [Pair-AI Dogfooding](../../workflows/pair-ai-dogfooding/) mode: one AI session as sparring product-team partner (empathy mapping → opportunity framing → evidence-backed prioritisation), a second as live observer/meta-PM. Three background research sweeps ([Researcher Workflow](../../workflows/researcher/)) supplied sourced evidence — German rental-market stats, the Bewerbermappe convention, expat pain points, and the competitive field. The run also *improved the product that ran it*: one feature shipped mid-session (`/mine`), one closed issue (#7 — naming on the way out, first exercised here when "renter-create-bewerbung" became "Land a flat in Germany without the German"), and a dozen backlog issues filed from live friction.
