# Fact-pack — "Solutions today" for expat renters getting their best shot at a Berlin flat

**Scope:** What exists TODAY (online + offline) to help expat / non-German-speaking renters across three jobs: (1) FINDING listings, (2) CREATING/assembling a rental application (Bewerbung / Bewerbermappe / Mieterselbstauskunft), (3) COMMUNICATING with landlords. Focus on what actually exists, who it's for, rough price, and the gap each leaves for a non-fluent expat assembling a credible, personalised Bewerbung fast.
**Date:** 2026-07-19
**Confidence key:** [V] = verified against a cited source · [⚠️] = unverified / uncertain (see final section)

**Take:** The portals have solved *discovery*. The two dominant local portals (ImmoScout24, WG-Gesucht) have each bolted on a paid "applicant profile / Bewerbermappe" feature — but it's a **document locker + visibility boost**, gated behind a paid tier, and it does NOT write a personalised, fluent German cover message.

## 2. Application-assembly tools (Job 2: CREATING the Bewerbung)

**What a German landlord pack ("Bewerbermappe") typically needs [V]:** passport/ID, employment contract, last 3 payslips, **SCHUFA credit report**, previous-landlord rent-payment confirmation (Mietschuldenfreiheitsbescheinigung), proof of liability insurance, and a completed **Mieterselbstauskunft** (tenant self-disclosure). Foreigners often also need certified translations and/or a German guarantor.
Source: [germaninsights checklist](https://germaninsights.com/documents-needed-to-rent-in-germany/) · [Handbook Germany](https://handbookgermany.de/en/search-a-flat)

- **SCHUFA reports:** free annual *Datenkopie* vs. paid rental-specific **SCHUFA-BonitätsAuskunft** (~€30, landlord-preferred, has a summary score) [V]. Free-Datenkopie workaround widely documented ([allaboutberlin/schufa](https://allaboutberlin.com/guides/schufa)).
- **bonify** (a SCHUFA subsidiary): free score app; its "tenant information" bundles a completed Selbstauskunft + rent-payment confirmation + credit report + proof of income into one document [V]. Closest existing thing to a one-click credibility pack — but German-centric and score/credit-led, not narrative. Source: [bonify Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bonifynative) · [findenglish SCHUFA-for-foreigners](https://www.findenglish.de/blog/schufa-for-foreigners-build-german-credit-score)
- **Mieterselbstauskunft templates:** free PDF/form templates everywhere (ImmoScout24, Wohnungsbörse, Paperform, Waitly) [V] — a blank form, not a filled, persuasive pack. Sources: [Paperform template](https://paperform.co/templates/german-rental-application-form-mietinteressenten-selbstauskunft/) · [Waitly example](https://waitly.eu/blog/mieterselbstauskunft-example-fill-it-out-correctly)
- **ImmoScout24 / WG-Gesucht digital folders:** as above — paid locker that stores the docs; assembly is still manual and the SCHUFA/income proof is the user's to obtain.

**Deposit-side / SCHUFA-substitute services** (help credibility when you lack SCHUFA/cash, adjacent to the Bewerbung):
- **Getmomo** — rent-deposit *guarantee* (issues a bank guarantee / Bankbürgschaft, monthly payable instead of 3-month cash) [V]. [getmomo.de/en](https://getmomo.de/en/tenant/)
- **Garentii** — deposit guarantee, "apply in 2 min," small fee instead of upfront cash deposit [V]. [garentii.com](https://www.garentii.com/de-en)
- Alternative-scoring note: some services use Infoscore/CRIF models kinder to expats with no German credit history [⚠️]. [findenglish](https://www.findenglish.de/blog/schufa-for-foreigners-build-german-credit-score)
- **Caveat [V]:** landlords may legally insist on cash deposit and private landlords often decline guarantee certificates — so these don't reliably substitute for a strong application.

**Gap:** No tool takes a non-fluent expat's raw facts (job, income, no-SCHUFA, foreign references) and outputs a *landlord-ready, culturally-correct, filled* Selbstauskunft + framing. Templates are blank; bonify is credit-led; portal folders are lockers.

## 3. Landlord communication / cover-letter help (Job 3: COMMUNICATING)

- **Portal message templates + priority messaging:** ImmoScout24 Plus "priority messaging" (your inquiry floats to top of inbox) and translation aid; WG-Gesucht+ "higher message visibility" [V]. These boost *placement*, not *persuasiveness or fluency*.
- **Bulk-apply / automation (grey area):**
  - **ImmoScout24 Apply Bot** (Apify) — auto-submits applications at speed via your account [V]. [apify.com/clearpath/immoscout24-apply-bot](https://apify.com/clearpath/immoscout24-apply-bot)
  - **nickirk/immo** (GitHub) — open-source bot monitoring ImmoScout24 + WG-Gesucht and auto-sending requests [V]. [github.com/nickirk/immo](https://github.com/nickirk/immo)
  - **n8n workflow template** — auto-search + filter + apply [V]. [n8n.io workflow 6150](https://n8n.io/workflows/6150-auto-germany-apartment-search-and-apply-with-immobilienscout24-and-google-services/)
  - These optimise *speed/volume* with a generic canned message — the opposite of a credible, personalised Bewerbung; likely against portal ToS [⚠️].
- **AI cover-letter generators (the closest direct competitors):**
  - **settlyo — Rental application letter generator** [V]: free, no account; produces a polite **German Anschreiben** for landlords **plus a document checklist**; part of a free expat toolkit (letter scanner, salary calc, CV builder). Recommends writing in German. → Most on-target free tool today. [settlyo.de/tools/rental-application](https://settlyo.de/tools/rental-application) · [settlyo.de](https://settlyo.de/)
  - **AnschreibenAI** — AI cover-letter generator, pay-per-use, but oriented to **job** applications not rentals [⚠️]. [anschreibenai.de/en](https://anschreibenai.de/en/)
  - **sharehouse.app** — AI rental cover-letter / reference tool (generic, non-German-market) [⚠️]. [sharehouse.app/ai](https://sharehouse.app/ai)
  - Generic AI (ChatGPT, LazyApply, AgentDock, etc.) — can draft, but user must supply market knowledge + verify tone.
- **Translation:** DeepL / Google Translate widely used to decode listings and draft messages; ImmoScout Plus bundles translation [V]. No rental-specific framing.

**Gap:** Only settlyo directly targets a *German rental Anschreiben*, and it's a single-shot free generator — no tie-in to the applicant's real profile/documents, no per-listing tailoring at scale, no coaching on what makes a Berlin landlord say yes.

## 4. Offline / human services (Job 1+2+3, done for you)

- **Relocation / flat-search agents (Berlin, expat-focused):**
  - **BerlinRelo** — done-for-you flat search; ~**€1,600 + VAT** avg fee; avg 53-day search, 4.4 viewings, 2.38 applications; 30-day refund [V]. [berlinrelo.de](https://www.berlinrelo.de/)
  - **FARAWAYHOME** — relocation packages (search + Anmeldung + bank + insurance + permits) from **€800** [V]. [farawayhome.com/en/berlin/relocation-agencies](https://www.farawayhome.com/en/berlin/relocation-agencies)
  - **Caselink** — "AI automation + human" relocation incl. apartment hunting [V]; **Expats in Wonderland**, **Archer Relocation**, **Arriva**, **FlatHunt** — expat apartment-search coaching/services [V]. [theberlinlife](https://theberlinlife.com/relocation-companies-in-berlin/) · [expatsinwonderland](https://www.expatsinwonderland.com/apartment-search)
  - These solve all three jobs but cost **hundreds to €1,600+** — out of reach for most individual renters and slow.
- **Word-of-mouth / community:** Facebook groups (e.g. "Berlin Apartments/Wohnungen"), r/germany, r/berlin, Toytown — free but noisy, scam-prone, no application help [⚠️].
- **Traditional Makler (buyer/tenant agent):** *Bestellerprinzip* means the commissioning party pays; tenant-paid Makler for rentals is now rare/limited by law [⚠️].

**Gap:** The only end-to-end "best shot" help is a €800–1,600 human agent. Nothing affordable/self-serve bridges FIND → ASSEMBLE → PERSUADE for a solo expat.

## Implications for the canvas — "Solutions today" box & remaining white space

1. **The portals own FINDING and have already tiptoed into ASSEMBLY.** ImmoScout24 Plus and WG-Gesucht+ both sell a "digital applicant profile / Bewerbermappe" — but it's a **paid document-locker + visibility boost**, not a fluent, persuasive, personalised application. The canvas should log these as the incumbent "solutions today," and note their feature is a *locker*, not a *writer*.

2. **CREATING a credible, filled Bewerbung is the least-served job.** Everything for assembly is either a **blank template** (Mieterselbstauskunft PDFs), a **credit artefact** (SCHUFA, bonify), or a **deposit substitute** (Getmomo/Garentii). Nothing turns an expat's raw, non-fluent facts into a landlord-ready, culturally-correct pack. **This is the sharpest white space for a "best shot" tool.**

3. **COMMUNICATING is served only shallowly.** Choices today are: canned portal templates + paid "priority" placement, ToS-risky bulk-apply bots (volume over quality), or one-shot AI generators (settlyo is the only German-rental-specific one). None personalise per-listing *at scale* or coach what wins a Berlin landlord. Gap = personalised, fluent, per-listing cover message tied to the applicant's real profile.

4. **The only end-to-end "best shot" today is a €800–1,600 human relocation agent** — proof the pain is real and monetisable, but priced out of reach for the individual solo renter. A self-serve product that delivers a fraction of that quality at consumer price is the value proposition.

5. **Non-fluency is the through-line no incumbent centres.** Translation (DeepL, ImmoScout Plus) decodes text but doesn't produce a *natively-credible German application*. A tool that combines assemble + personalise + fluent-German + expat-context (no SCHUFA, foreign references, guarantor framing) would sit in genuinely open space across all three jobs — most defensibly around **CREATE**.
