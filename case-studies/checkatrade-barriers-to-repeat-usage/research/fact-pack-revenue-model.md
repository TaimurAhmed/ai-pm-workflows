# Fact-pack: How Checkatrade makes money (researched 2026-07-05)

*Researcher Workflow output. Sources: join.checkatrade.com/pricing, /sponsored-listings, /priced-services, /directory, checkatrade.com blog (June 2025 product release), Companies House, Wikipedia. Verified vs unverified marked.*

## Core revenue — tradespeople pay subscriptions
- **Free** £0/mo — profile + reviews only, no leads, no vetted tick.
- **Approved** £30/mo (+VAT) — the "Checkatrade tick" (up to 12 background checks), review/quoting tools, directory inclusion.
- **Growth** from £59/mo — everything above **plus leads: trade picks postcodes + annual lead volume and pays a fixed monthly price for that volume** (quote-based by trade/area; bespoke packages for firms spending £1,000+/mo). Leads are bundled into subscription, not billed per lead.
- No commission on ordinary jobs; no joining fee.

## New transactional streams (2025 →)
- **Priced Services** (Autumn '25): bookable fixed-price services; **dynamic service fee charged to the trade per confirmed booking** (fee schedule ⚠️ unverified).
- **Checkatrade Pay**: payment processing **1.29–1.79%**, usable on any job (even off-platform ones). Card pre-auth for Priced Services; funds released on completion.
- **Checkatrade Shop** (boilers, solar) and **financing** via Creditec.

## Secondary
- **Sponsored Listings**: PPC auction for top-2 search slots, from £100/mo.
- **Print directory** ads (24M households, 300+ areas).
- Partner benefits (insurance, QuickBooks, Wickes) — whether Checkatrade earns referral revenue ⚠️ unverified.

## Consumers
- Free to search/review/book. **£1,000 job guarantee** on jobs booked through the platform. Money can now flow through the platform via Checkatrade Pay.

## Ownership & strategy
- Operating co: **Vetted Ltd** (CH 4285394), Portsmouth. HomeServe acquired 2017 → **Brookfield** ultimate parent (2022/23). CEO **Jambu Palaniappan** (ex-Uber) since Sept 2023.
- **Stated strategy (their words, June 2025):** *"evolving from a trusted directory into an end-to-end home improvement marketplace"* — monetisation shifting toward transactions (booking, payments, commerce, financing).
- Scale markers: ~48,000 member trades; £55.6m revenue 2022 (⚠️ Wikipedia; latest accounts not verified).

## Implication for the canvas (box 5)
Repeat homeowner usage now drives revenue through **three reinforcing channels**: (1) lead demand → Growth-tier upgrades/retention, (2) **transaction fees that scale directly with jobs** (Priced Services + Pay — the strategic growth engine), (3) sponsored-listing demand. Repeat usage is the demand-side engine of the exact marketplace transition the CEO is publicly driving.
