## Their jargon (use their words)
- Supply: **"trades" / "tradespeople"**; paying trades = **"members"** with a **"membership"**. Demand: **"homeowners"**.
- Vetting: **"12 checks" / "12-point vetting process"**, "vetted", "continually rechecked"; **"The Checkatrade Standard"** = six quality commitments.
- **"Checkatrade Guarantee"** — up to £1,000 on jobs booked through the platform.
- Monetisation: **"leads"**, "job enquiries", "win more work", **"Priced Services"** ("Get it done, for a fixed price… Book instantly"), **"Checkatrade Pay"**, **"Sponsored Listings"**, **"TradeMore"** (job-management tool), the print **"Directory"**.
- Self-description: **"the UK's leading platform for home improvement."**

## Strategy signals (their words)
- *"Our goal is to become the go-to platform in the UK for researching tradespeople… extending our platform to include profiles for all tradespeople, and not just our members."* → widening the directory (supply liquidity + SEO) while the product motion (Pay, Priced Services, Guarantee) pushes **directory → transactional marketplace**.

## Public metrics
- **"6M+ real reviews"**, "12 checks", £1,000 guarantee (homepage, Jul 2026). ~**50,000 members** (2022 statement ⚠️ dated). 668 rogue trades rejected in 6 months (2024 PR). Founded 1998, Selsey; HQ Portsmouth.
